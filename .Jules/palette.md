## 2026-03-11 - Explicit Keyboard Discoverability
**Learning:** Terminal applications often support standard readline-style shortcuts (like Ctrl+u to cut to start, Ctrl+k to cut to end), but users don't assume they are available.
**Action:** Explicitly surface standard keyboard shortcuts in help menus or shortcut overlays to improve discoverability, rather than assuming users will try them automatically. Use clear language like 'to cut to start' instead of jargon like 'kill to start'.
