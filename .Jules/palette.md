## 2024-05-14 - Add ctrl+u and ctrl+k to tui2 shortcuts
**Learning:** Terminal users often don't know that standard readline shortcuts work in custom TUIs unless explicitly told. Discoverability is key.
**Action:** Expose `Ctrl+u` (cut to start) and `Ctrl+k` (cut to end) in the footer shortcut overlay to make these text-editing capabilities visible.
