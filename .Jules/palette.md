## 2024-08-02 - Accessible Redirect Countdown
**Learning:** Dynamic text changes (like a countdown) are not automatically announced by screen readers if they are not in an aria-live region. Simply toggling `display: none` to `display: flex` on a container with an `aria-live` attribute can sometimes fail to announce subsequent text updates reliably depending on the screen reader. Also, abbreviations like '3s' are often not read intuitively; spelling out '3 seconds' with proper pluralization ('1 second') provides a much clearer audio experience.
**Action:** When implementing countdowns or frequently changing text, place a visually hidden element (`.sr-only`) with `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"` permanently in the DOM. Update its `textContent` alongside the visual element. Always spell out time units and implement proper pluralization to ensure the best auditory UX.
