## 2024-05-15 - Terminal Shortcuts Discoverability
**Learning:** Standard terminal keyboard shortcuts (like `Ctrl+u` to cut to start of line, or `Ctrl+k` to cut to end of line) should be explicitly surfaced in help menus and UI overlays, rather than assuming users will try them automatically. This improves accessibility and discoverability, especially for users who might not be deep terminal power users.
**Action:** When working on terminal applications, proactively add common readline/terminal shortcuts to the visual help UI.
