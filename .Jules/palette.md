## 2026-03-02 - Explicitly Surfacing Terminal Shortcuts
**Learning:** Terminal users often aren't aware of standard readline-like shortcuts (Ctrl+U, Ctrl+K) in custom TUIs unless explicitly surfaced in the UI. Assuming standard behavior leads to discoverability issues.
**Action:** Explicitly surface standard terminal keyboard shortcuts (like Ctrl+u and Ctrl+k for deletion) in help menus or shortcut overlays to improve discoverability.
