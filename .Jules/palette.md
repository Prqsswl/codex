## 2024-05-19 - Discoverability of standard deletion shortcuts
**Learning:** Standard terminal deletion shortcuts like `Ctrl+u` (cut to start) and `Ctrl+k` (cut to end) were already supported in `TextArea`, but their discoverability was very poor without explicitly showing them in the help/shortcut overlay. Users might not realize standard terminal interactions are supported. Exposing these existing shortcuts in the visual UI instantly improves usability for keyboard-centric users.
**Action:** Always surface existing, powerful keyboard shortcuts in help menus or shortcut overlays, even if they are standard convention. Never assume users will try them automatically in a new application context. Use consistent language like "cut to start" rather than "kill to start" to improve general user comprehension.
