## 2024-05-14 - Expose Built-in Terminal Deletion Shortcuts
**Learning:** Terminal-native deletion shortcuts (like `Ctrl+u` and `Ctrl+k`) are powerful but often lack discoverability for users accustomed to graphical text editors. Relying on implicit knowledge means many users miss out on efficient navigation and editing.
**Action:** Always explicitly document and surface these standard terminal shortcuts in help overlays or tooltips to bridge the gap between advanced terminal users and beginners.
