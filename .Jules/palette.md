## 2024-05-24 - Document Text Area Commands in TUI Shortcuts
**Learning:** Discoverability of standard line editing commands (`Ctrl+u`, `Ctrl+k`) is crucial for command-line text input tools, as users may not immediately assume standard keybindings are supported.
**Action:** When working on terminal UIs, ensure standard text-editing shortcuts are prominently displayed in context-aware overlay menus to support smooth user interactions.
