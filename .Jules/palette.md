## 2024-05-22 - TUI Keybinding Discoverability
**Learning:** Hidden powerful shortcuts (like Emacs bindings in TextAreas) are often present but unused because they are invisible. Exposing them in the footer overlay is a low-cost, high-value way to educate users without cluttering the primary UI.
**Action:** Always check component capabilities (like `TextArea`) for unexposed shortcuts and add them to the help/shortcut overlay if available.
