## 2024-05-22 - Discoverability of Hidden Power User Features
**Learning:** TUI text areas often support Emacs-style keybindings (e.g., Ctrl+k, Ctrl+u) natively, but these are invisible to users. Explicitly listing at least one 'power user' shortcut (like Ctrl+k) in the help footer hints at the existence of others and significantly improves discoverability for advanced editing without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** When reviewing TUI applications, check for hidden 'power user' bindings in text input components and expose them in help menus to bridge the gap between basic and advanced usage.
